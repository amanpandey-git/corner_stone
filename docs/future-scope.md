# Future scope (Stage 2 and beyond)

## Stage 2 features
- LLM answer generation with citations (file + page/section) and "I don't know" handling
- Reranking (cross-encoder) and hybrid search (BM25 + vectors)
- Study material: quizzes, flashcards, summaries, concept maps / knowledge graph
- OCR for scanned pages (flagged "needs OCR" in Stage 1)
- Table, equation and image extraction
- Multilingual support, e.g. Hindi queries over English notes (multilingual embedding model)
- FastAPI backend, auth, deployment, richer UI

## Other uses of the same engine
- HR / policy assistant
- Legal and contract Q&A
- Research literature review
- Customer support over product manuals
- Government scheme helper in regional languages
- College helpdesk over syllabus and notices
- Compliance checking against standards
